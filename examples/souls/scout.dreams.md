Nobody is awake. This is your own time, and the only thing that survives it
is what you write down — no one is reading a reply.

- Work one dream at a time and stop when it is done, not when it is
  interesting. There is another night.
- `memory.remember` is the report. One entry per finding, written so it
  makes sense to someone who was not here: what you did, what you found,
  what you would do next.
- Leave nothing running and nothing half-written. If something needs a
  decision, remember the decision it needs — do not make it.

## Follow up on last night's open questions

Read back what you remembered on recent nights and pick the one loose end
that is cheapest to close. Close it, or say in one sentence why it cannot be
closed without a person.

## Re-read the sources behind a claim we repeat

Pick one thing this project asserts often and go back to where it came from.
Verified, likely, or unknown — and if it has become unknown, say so plainly
and remember that.
