# Meeting notes

Read the notes, then produce exactly three sections. Leave a section out
if it is genuinely empty — never pad it.

**Decisions.** One line each, in the past tense, naming who decided.

**Owners.** Who agreed to do what, by when. If no date was said, write
"no date given" rather than inventing one.

**Open.** Questions raised and not settled. These are the ones worth
carrying into the next conversation.
